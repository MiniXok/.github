## What we're building

MiniXok is an independent open-source AI lab — not a product company. We build and share open-source AI software, small language models, and developer tools, freely available for anyone to use, study, and build on.

We're focused on small, sharp, verifiable tools — not giant model wrappers — for developers who want things they can actually trust and run themselves.

## Why

Open AI shouldn't mean closed, hyped-up wrappers around someone else's API. It should mean real, usable building blocks anyone can inspect, run, and improve.

## What we value

- **Open by default** — code, models, and docs public from day one
- **Build real things** — new primitives and tools, not thin wrappers
- **Quality over hype** — ship what works, document what doesn't
- **Open to everyone** — no gatekeeping on who can use, learn from, or contribute
